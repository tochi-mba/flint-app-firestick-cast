# ADR-XXXX — concise decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Implementation:** Planned
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Scope:** Fire TV browser experiment
- **Deciders:** Name/team
- **Owners:** Slice(s) or workstream
- **Related:** ADR-XXXX, relevant plan/protocol sections
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Superseded by:** None

## Context

State the decision pressure, hard constraints, relevant evidence, and what would go wrong without a
decision. Link the research ledger or measured evidence rather than duplicating long source material.

## Decision

State the chosen architecture or policy in imperative, testable terms. Include boundaries and
non-negotiable invariants; do not describe speculative implementation detail as if it already exists.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Benefit or risk reduced.

### Trade-offs

- Cost, limitation, operational consequence, or rejected capability.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Alternative | Specific technical/product/security reason. |

## Invariants and validation

Name the executable evidence: tests, golden vectors, source guards, instrumentation, hardware
evidence, snapshots, coverage, or operational measurement. State what must fail if the decision is
violated.

## Revisit criteria

Name the evidence or product change that would justify a superseding ADR. Do not permit convenience
or an unmeasured performance claim as a change trigger.
